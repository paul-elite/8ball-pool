Energy & XP
Every legal pot earns XP and fills your energy bar: +20 per ball, plus bonuses for multi-pots, bank shots, kick shots, long pots and streaks. Fouls drain 25. A full bar earns the next boost: Laser, Freeze, Ghost, Time Warp, Rewind, then repeat. Everyone starts with one Laser Aim.
1 · Laser AimLong guideline with up to 5 cushion bounces for the rest of your turn
2 · FreezeCue ball stops dead on first contact
3 · Ghost BallCue ball passes straight through the first ball it touches
4 · Time WarpSlow motion. If you pot nothing, you keep the table (fouls still count)
5 · RewindInsurance: if the shot loses your turn, it's undone and you shoot again
